Window & behaviour

To give Cheese its own desktop: open Mission Control (swipe up with 3 fingers / F3), drag the Cheese window onto the "+" desktop at the top right. Then a partial 4-finger swipe peeks at it without switching. Once sharing starts, Cheese never takes focus, so macOS never jumps to its desktop.
